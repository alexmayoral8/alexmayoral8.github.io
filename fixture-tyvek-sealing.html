Medical Tray Sealing Alignment Fixture

Mechanical fixture designed to support repeatable tray positioning and Tyvek alignment during the medical device packaging heat-sealing process.

Overview

This project involved the mechanical design of a fixture used in the packaging process of medical devices. The fixture was designed to position medical trays, align Tyvek sheets over them, and support the placement of the assembly into a heat-sealing machine.

Problem

Accurate alignment between the medical tray and Tyvek is critical during the sealing process to ensure consistent seal quality and reduce process variation. Manual alignment can increase the risk of misalignment, rework, and operator-dependent variability.

Solution

A mechanical fixture was designed with a rigid base, tray positioning cavities, alignment references, and fastening components. The fixture allowed multiple trays to be positioned repeatably while helping operators align the Tyvek before the sealing process.

What I Did

  • Designed the mechanical fixture concept for medical tray positioning.
  • Created an assembly design to define tray cavities and Tyvek alignment references.
  • Included fastening and locating features to support repeatable operator setup.
  • Built and evaluated the fixture in the real packaging workflow.
