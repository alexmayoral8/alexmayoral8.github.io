Manufacturing Alignment Fixture for Medical Device Assembly

Custom fixture designed to guide and align two medical device components before bonding, improving repeatability and reducing the risk of defective assemblies.

Overview

This project involved the design of a custom fixture to improve a manufacturing step for a medical device assembly. The process required bonding and aligning two components, where manual positioning could easily lead to misalignment. Once bonded, the components could not be separated without damaging the assembly, resulting in scrap.

To address this issue, I designed a fixture that helped guide and align both components before bonding, making the process easier, more repeatable, and less dependent on manual positioning. The fixture was developed to support a faster and more consistent assembly workflow while reducing the risk of defective parts.

My Role

I contributed to the mechanical design of the fixture, including the alignment features, CAD development, and iterative design improvements from the preliminary version to the final CAD design.

Skills Applied

  • Fixture design
  • CAD design
  • Manufacturing support
  • Process improvement
  • Alignment control
  • Medical device assembly
  • Design for repeatability

Impact

The fixture helped simplify the alignment process, reduce the risk of misaligned assemblies, and support a more efficient manufacturing step.
