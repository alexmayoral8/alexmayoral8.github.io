Fixture for fatigue test

Automated fixture to reproduce cyclic deformation in a therapy device, validating its mechanical integrity and operation after repeated use.

Project Overview

During my internship at a medical device company, I designed and developed a custom test fixture to perform fatigue testing on a wearable device intended to relieve abdominal pain. The fixture was created to simulate the repetitive bending experienced by the device during normal use, enabling long-term durability and reliability evaluation under controlled conditions.

The fixture uses an electro-mechanical system with stepper motors, Arduino control and specific fasteners to flex both ends of the device in an automated and consistent manner.

My contributions

  • Designed the fixture using CAD software.
  • Developed a mechanism to reproduce cyclic bending conditions.
  • Selected manufacturing processes and materials.
  • Fabricated and assembled the fixture using laser-cut acrylic and 3D-printed components.
  • Created a fatigue testing protocol.
  • Defined pre-test and post-test inspection procedures.
  • Executed the test protocol with 10 devices.
  • Made a test report with the findings.
