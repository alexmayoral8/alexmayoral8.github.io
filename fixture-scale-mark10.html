Scale Fixture for Mark-10 Cutting Force Test

Custom fixture developed to support repeatable force measurement during a medical device thread-cutting mechanical test.

Overview

This project involved designing a custom fixture to support a mechanical test for a medical device. The objective of the test was to measure the force required for the device to cut a thread using a Mark-10 test frame operating at a controlled speed.

To capture the required force, a scale had to be integrated into the test setup. A custom fixture was designed to securely position the scale on the test frame, enabling a stable and repeatable testing configuration.

Problem

The test required a reliable way to mount a scale onto the Mark-10 frame while maintaining alignment and stability. Without a proper fixture, the setup would be difficult to reproduce and could affect the consistency of the force measurements.

Solution

A custom mechanical fixture was developed to hold and position the scale within the test setup. This enabled controlled and repeatable measurement of the cutting force required by the medical device.

What I Did

  • Designed the fixture to locate and support the scale on the Mark-10 frame.
  • Created a stable setup for controlled cutting-force measurement.
  • Improved repeatability by reducing manual positioning variability.
  • Supported mechanical verification testing for a medical device.
